# Acknowledgements / 致谢

## dayGLANCE and its contributors

Jobo is derived from [dayGLANCE](https://github.com/krelltunez/dayGLANCE), created and maintained upstream by [krelltunez](https://github.com/krelltunez) with contributions from its community.

The existing planner, task and calendar models, views, themes, integrations, localisation infrastructure, build tooling and test suite are inherited work. They are not presented as original Jobo inventions. We are grateful for that foundation and for the reviews and design discussion around the Jobo prototypes.

感谢 krelltunez 与 dayGLANCE 社区提供原始项目、功能基础及评审建议。Jobo 不把继承的代码、设计与基础设施归为自己的原创成果，也不把独立维护表述为上游的官方授权发行或维护职责委派。

## Jobo contributions and stewardship

[Lcub3d](https://github.com/Lcub3d) is responsible for this independent Jobo fork's product direction, ongoing maintenance and release decisions. Its initial integrated work includes the Simplified Chinese contributions, selective Todoist integration and the desktop/mobile Plan / Do prototypes listed in [the provenance record](docs/jobo-maintenance.md).

Git history and original PR links are retained. Upstream review, changes and later improvements remain attributable to their respective contributors. This repository's maintenance arrangement is separate from upstream dayGLANCE governance; no upstream core-maintainer role or endorsement is implied.

## AI assistance

Implementation, tests and documentation have been developed with AI assistance, including ChatGPT and Codex. AI assistance does not transfer maintenance responsibility to an upstream contributor and is not a substitute for code review, reproducible tests, data-safety checks or accurate disclosure of limitations.

产品工作流和交互方向由维护者决定，代码、测试与文档使用 AI 辅助完成。采用 AI 不代表代码已自动达到正式发布标准；维护、核查和发布责任仍属于本分支维护者。

## Continuing cooperation

Jobo is maintained independently so its workflow can evolve at its own pace. Suitable improvements may still be offered upstream as focused contributions. Upstream adoption, scope and release timing remain the upstream project's decisions. The existing Jobo PRs stay available for review independently of this fork's development.
