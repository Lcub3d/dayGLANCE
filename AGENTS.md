# Legacy branch: use jobo-main

This `main` branch is retained for history and as a repository entry page. It is not the development base for new Jobo features.

Before starting Jobo work, fetch and switch to `origin/jobo-main`, then read that branch's AGENTS.md, CONTRIBUTING.md and docs/jobo-maintenance.md. Create new feature branches from `jobo-main`, not this legacy branch.

Do not follow historical upstream-first or automatic-PR instructions in `.upstream.md` snapshots. Do not delete, rename or force-push `dayGLANCE-jobo` or `feature/jobo-mobile-plan-do`; they are the sources of the still-separate upstream Jobo PRs. Do not modify or close upstream PRs without an explicit owner request.

Keep the original MIT license, attribution and history. Retiring the old development line does not authorise archiving the repository, removing branches or changing application data.
