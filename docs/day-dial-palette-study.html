Dial Palette Study

Real colours through the app's own pipeline: muteDialColor, then dialIntensity by duration, then the state multipliers. Each widget is 364×382pt. Compare on the phone.

What's being compared

A is what the widget gets if Phase 2 ports the app treatment literally. The rim is 2 to 4 units in a 1000-unit viewBox, so it comes out at 0.8 to 1.5pt here. B gives the rim a minimum weight and raises the fills 2.5× so short blocks don't disappear. C raises the fills further, 3.5×, so the band reads close to solid but keeps the lit rim. D is the solid band from the spec you approved, now in the real colours. The colour source, the duration curve and the state logic are the same in all four. Only the fill and rim weights change.

Past blocks follow the app, not the spec. The app dims by state: completed blocks fade, including the rim, while past blocks left undone keep a full-strength rim. In the dense day that's the 09:00 red Todoist block. So the app already marks unfinished work, and the spec's plain 42% time dimming would lose that.

The projected-day problem

Switch to Projected, as-is. A projected day can't know what gets completed, so every flag arrives as false. Under the app's rules, every past block then shows as left undone. Each morning before you open the app, the widget would mark everything you already finished as unfinished. Projected, neutral past uses the app's past-event multipliers (0.6 / 0.6) instead, which say "this happened" without claiming anything about completion.
